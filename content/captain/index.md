Most Saturdays, two volunteer captains pick teams one by one and play a fixed-roster match,
usually with far more players than a normal round. It is the best place to learn how a big
base is actually built, and the coin bonus rates are the highest in the game.

Between those events, **BB Captains** runs captains games on demand: sign up in the BB Captains
lobby (in game or on the website), volunteer as captain if you like, and the game is called as
soon as enough players and two captains are waiting — see [BB Captains](bb-captains.md).
