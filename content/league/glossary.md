Every term below is used by the availability grid, the team meter and the bot messages.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Slot** | One of the fixed time blocks in the grid (e.g. *Tue 18:00*). Slot names are **Central European** (Paris, CET/CEST) and are the same every week — they are the only times on the site not converted to your own timezone. Once a slot becomes a real dated match, that time *is* shown in your timezone. |
| **Mark** | Your answer on a slot: **✓** available · **~** if needed · **✗** no. Both ✓ and ~ count as "I can play". |
| **Commit** | Ticking *"I commit…"* and pressing **Save & commit**. **Uncommitted grids are ignored at the lock** — saving a draft is not enough. |
| **Common slot** | A slot marked by **at least 3 committed teammates** (3 players make a match). Your own ticks alone never create one — this is why the meter can show fewer commons than you expect. **You need 4.** |
| **Anchor band** | A deliberately narrow set of slots: the **weekday 18:00** column, or the **weekend** slots (Sat/Sun 14:00, 15:00, 22:00). Your 4 commons must include **2 inside one band**. Why: if every team spreads its availability thin, two teams never land on the same slot. Concentration is what makes matches happen — *one* band is enough, pick whichever fits your timezone. |
| **Bonus slot** | Sat/Sun **16:00 / 18:00 / 20:00**, tagged *bonus*. They only exist on dates with **no captain games** (weekend evenings normally belong to the captain tournament). They **do** count as common slots and matches **can** be played on them — they just don't count towards the anchor. |
| **Why weekends differ from weekdays** | Not a preference: the captain tournament owns weekend evenings, so 3v3 historically only had 14:00/15:00/22:00 there. The weekday 18:00 column is the mirror band for people who can't play weekends. |
| **Weekend 14:00 / 15:00** | You may tick **both** if you're free at both. They count as **one** common slot, and only one match can be scheduled across them — a single server can't host two games an hour apart. |
| **Gate** | The check run at the lock: **3+ committed members**, **4+ common slots**, **2 in an anchor band**. Pass it and you're paired. |
| **Rescue** | If you miss the *anchor* only, but the scheduler can still find you a slot against an opponent, **you're paired anyway** — no joker burned. The volume (4 commons) and the 3 committed members are still required. |
| **Joker** | **One per team for the whole tournament.** Missing the gate spends it: you skip the round with **no win and no loss**. Miss it again with the joker gone → forfeit. |
| **Bye** | A round without an opponent. With an odd number of teams it's a **free win**; when it comes from a failed gate it's the joker (neutral). |
| **Forfeit** | Counted as a **loss**: gate missed with the joker already spent. |
| **Lock** | **Sunday evening** — the exact instant, in your own timezone, is on the 🔒 lock chip at the top of the Cup page and above the grid. Grids freeze, pairings and match times are computed automatically, everyone gets a DM. After that, only captains can reschedule. |
| **Blueberry muffin** | Not a tournament term. If you got this far, you read the entire glossary — respect. 🫐 (250g flour, 100g sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 egg, 240ml milk, 80g melted butter, 150g blueberries — 200°C, 22 min.) |
| **Commitment minimum** | To commit you need **8 marks incl. 4 firm ✓** — or, if your availability is structurally smaller: **all 4 weekend slots** (3 firm), or **all 5 slots of a single weekday** (4 firm). It's an anti-troll floor, never a reason to exclude someone genuinely busy. |
