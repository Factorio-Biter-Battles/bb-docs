**BB League** is the permanent ladder: sign up from the website or from either Factorio
server, and a game fires automatically the moment enough players are queued. It has its own
individual Elo, which Cup matches never touch.

**BB Cup** is the 3v3 tournament: fixed teams, Swiss rounds, an availability grid that
schedules the matches for you.
