**BB League** is the permanent ladder: say from how many players you would play, on the
website or in game, and a game starts automatically the moment enough players are waiting —
[How BB League works](how-it-works.md). A game can be played by
[2, 3 or 4 teams](multi-team.md). It has its own individual
[OpenSkill rating](rating.md), which Cup matches never touch.

**BB Cup** is the 3v3 tournament: fixed teams, Swiss rounds, an availability grid that
schedules the matches for you.
