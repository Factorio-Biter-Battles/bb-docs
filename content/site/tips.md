## Quick tips

- Rush nades, flamers, or lasers — all viable survival paths (land mines are nerfed to
  −90 % on BB: don't bother).
- Type `askbot help` in-game for stats and trading commands.
- Your ELO is tracked when you play — but it's just for fun.

## Then read this

If you have ten minutes, the pages that pay for themselves fastest are, in order:

1. [Game basics](../gameplay/basics.md) — what evolution and threat actually do. Nothing
   else makes sense before this one.
2. [New-player strategy](../gameplay/strategy.md) — defense first, and the list of things
   that keep the silo alive.
3. [What not to do](../gameplay/mistakes.md) — the mistakes that annoy a team, each with the
   reason behind it.

And [joining, spectator and trust](../gameplay/joining.md) if the server asks you for
something you don't have yet.
