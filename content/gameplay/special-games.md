For captain-games scoring, picking strategy and roles see the
[Captain Games section](../captain/captain-games.md). This section covers the special-game
variants admins can spin up.

## Special games

Variant rule sets — restricted/unrestricted tech, free items, threat tweaks, etc. — set up
by admins, often voted by the players online. Announced in `#bb-special` on Discord.

## Free items

Most common addition. Lets thin teams reach late-game faster. Typical: armor + power +
personal roboport + bots in **infinity chests** near each silo. You can't interact with the
chest directly — drop an inserter onto it.

*Belt trick to grab tons quickly:* two belts facing each other create jitter; you can grab
items off an inserter as soon as it picks one up (before the place animation completes).
Holding mouse on the inserter's edge while jittering between the two belts → hundreds of
items in seconds.

## Moat games

- **Defensive moat** — water around main; early defense becomes trivial; usually goes to
  many rockets even on easy. Landfill carefully (small gaps so players cross but biters
  don't). **Big biter corpses become landfill** when killed → can bridge your moat → don't
  put lasers/turrets in vulnerable spots.
- **Outposting moat** — moats with empty (non-landfillable) gaps that force you through
  biter nests to reach outposts.

## Captain games (recap)

Most Saturdays at 17:00 Central European time — the Discord event shows it in your own
timezone. Captains pick teams one by one, hold final say on strategy. Listen or get booted.
Often 50+ players, well-balanced into late-game; experienced players in main and outposts →
great place to learn building. Community-picks games happen when no captains volunteer
(every player ranks every player; teams auto-balanced).
