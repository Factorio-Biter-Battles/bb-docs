BB Coins are earned by playing on the winning team; BB Bets is what you can do with them —
binary and multi-outcome markets on the live game, paying 1c per winning share.

Coins cannot be bought, sold or cashed out for anything. They exist to make watching and
playing more interesting, and everything about them — including the mistakes made along the
way — is written down.
